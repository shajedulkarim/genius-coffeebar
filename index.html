Our story

A little bit of everything,
a whole lot of happiness.

Genius is a coffee bar built on a simple idea: a good cup, a warm welcome and a place that makes the day feel a little brighter. Whether you stop by for a quick coffee on the go or settle in with something sweet, every order is made fresh.

From our signature lattes to icy fruit coolers and handmade desserts, the menu is small enough to be careful and wide enough to surprise you.
